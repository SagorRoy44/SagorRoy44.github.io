§02  About

Profile

Master's student in ICT with research experience in machine learning, bioinformatics, and AI-driven healthcare — including a peer-reviewed conference publication. I'm seeking to pursue graduate research applying computational methods to biomedical and agricultural problems.

I'm currently studying in the Department of Information and Communication Technology at Mawlana Bhashani Science and Technology University (MBSTU), where my undergraduate thesis applied machine learning and systems biology to precision breast-cancer biomarker discovery. Outside research, I build small IoT and software projects — from a Sudoku solver to an ESP32-based smart-home system — and in quieter hours I keep a garden and read.
